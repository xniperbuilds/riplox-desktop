      If YouTube starts asking whether you are a bot

      This is the one thing watching can cause. It is usually temporary and it does not break anything — ordinary downloads keep working. In order:

      Set Check every to 24 hours or 2 days, and remove any channel you do not really follow. Every watched item is another request.

      Turn Watch off for a day. These blocks lift on their own far more often than not.

      Settings → Sign-in → Sign in to YouTube. A signed-in request is rarely challenged. Use an account you do not mind losing — heavy automated use can get the account itself limited, and that is a real risk, not a formality.

      Settings → YouTube → proof-of-origin helper. It answers the check YouTube is making, which is exactly what this failure is.

      Settings → Advanced → Update engine. When YouTube changes something, the fix usually arrives there first.

      Riplox

      Read this before turning watching on

      • This is automated. Riplox will ask the site for each watched channel's newest videos on a timer, on its own, for as long as it is on.
      • Sites push back on that. YouTube can answer a repeated automated request with “Sign in to confirm you're not a bot”. If you have signed in through Riplox, heavy use can get that account limited — not just this app.
      • Nothing downloads by itself. Riplox only shows you what is new. Every download is still a button you press.
      • It is kept as small as it can be: the newest 30 items only, one check at a time, at least 90 seconds apart, and once every 12 hours per channel by default.
      • If it does happen, the fix is on this page, under “If YouTube starts asking whether you are a bot”. It is usually temporary, and ordinary downloads keep working.
