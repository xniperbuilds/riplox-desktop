          Read this before turning watching on
          
    
            
      
          • This is automated. Riplox will ask the site for each watched
        channel's newest videos on a timer, on its own, for as long as it is
        on.
            • 
      
          • Sites push back on that. YouTube can answer a repeated
        automated request with “Sign in to confirm you're not a bot”. If you
        have signed in through Riplox, heavy use can get that account
        limited — not just this app.
            • 
      
          • Nothing downloads by itself. Riplox only shows you what is
        new. Every download is still a button you press.
            • 
      
          • It is kept as small as it can be: the newest 30 items only,
        one check at a time, at least 90 seconds apart, and once every 12 hours
        per channel by default.
            • 
      
          • If it does happen, the fix is on this page, under “If
        YouTube starts asking whether you are a bot”. It is usually
        temporary, and ordinary downloads keep working.
            •
