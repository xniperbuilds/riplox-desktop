5. WHAT RIPLOX SENDS AND DOES NOT SEND

Riplox has no accounts, no telemetry and no analytics. It does not collect,
transmit or sell any personal information, and your download history stays on
your own computer. Unless you open the Board, described in section 6,
XniperBuilds receives nothing from your use of the program - and what the Board
receives is set out there rather than summarised away here.

Riplox makes network connections only for these purposes:

  a) the website you asked it to download from, or a page you asked it
     to read so that it can list the videos on it;
  b) once a day, a check with GitHub for a newer version of Riplox itself.
     This can be turned off in Settings. It only ever tells you: Riplox does
     not replace itself;
  c) keeping the download engine current. Riplox checks whether a newer
     engine has been published and fetches it on its own, because an engine
     that has fallen behind is the commonest reason a site stops working.
     This can be turned off in Settings, and Update there does the same
     thing by hand;
  d) the optional helper that some sites require. It is switched on by
     default, so a new install downloads it - about 44 MB, from its own
     release page - and checks it against a pinned SHA-256 before running
     it. It then runs entirely on your own computer, can be turned off in
     Settings, and removed there in one click;
  e) if you sign in through Riplox for private videos, the sign-in goes to
     that site and the session is stored encrypted on this machine only;
  f) if you turn on Sharing, an encrypted message sent between your own paired
     devices. It carries what you chose to send and nothing else: a link, or a
     short piece of text such as a key, a password or a Wi-Fi code. Whichever
     it is, it is sealed on the device that sends it and can only be opened by
     the device you paired it with - nobody in between, including XniperBuilds,
     can read it. The content of a video never passes through anyone else's
     server;
  g) if you open the Board, a connection to a server run by XniperBuilds for
     that one purpose, described in section 6. Nothing is sent while you are
     not looking at the Board, and a copy of Riplox whose Board is never
     opened never contacts it at all.

If you turn on the optional sponsor-segment feature, Riplox asks a public
community database about the video you are downloading. If you would rather
nothing was asked, leave that feature off.

Your settings, your saved sign-ins, your download history and anything still
waiting in the queue are kept in your own user folder on this computer, and
they are yours. Uninstalling Riplox does not delete them. The uninstaller asks
whether you want them removed as well, and the answer it assumes is no - so
uninstalling and installing again, which is the usual way of fixing something,
leaves everything exactly as you left it. Only if you deliberately answer yes
is that folder erased. Files you have already downloaded are never touched
either way, because they are saved wherever you chose and are not Riplox's to
remove.


6. THE BOARD

The Board is one public room inside Riplox where people post links. It is the
only part of the program that sends anything to XniperBuilds, and everything
about it happens only while you have it open.

WHAT IT IS. Links, and nothing else. There is no message box, no profile, no
reply, and no way to write a sentence of your own. Only YouTube, TikTok,
Instagram, X and Snapchat Spotlight links are accepted; a link to anywhere else
is refused rather than posted.

WHAT IS SENT WHEN YOU POST. The link, rebuilt. The server reads the platform
and the content id out of what you pasted and builds a clean address from its
own table, so the address you typed - including anything attached to it that
was tracking you - is neither stored nor shown to anybody. The title of the
video goes with it, read by your own computer from a site you had already
chosen to open, so that nobody reading the Board has to fetch anything from
that platform. A random identifier for this copy of Riplox is sent as well; it
is stored only as a salted hash, it is not a name or an account and is not
derived from anything about your machine, and deleting it gives you a new one.
As with any request to any website, your network address reaches the server
that answers. It is kept only as a salted hash, for counting how often one
place is posting, and it is never shown to anybody or stored beside anything
that could name you.

WHAT IS PUBLIC, AND FOR HOW LONG. Everything you post. Anyone running Riplox
with the Board open sees it, and links posted there are meant to stay. A link
is never deleted, including by you - so post one only if you are content for it
to be readable indefinitely.

WHAT YOU MAY POST. Only links you would be willing to hand to a stranger:
lawful where you are, yours to share, and honestly described. Do not post
anything unlawful, anything sexual involving a child, anything meant to harass
a person, or a link whose title describes something other than what it leads
to.

REPORTING. Every row has Report. The moment you press it, that row is hidden
for you, without waiting for anybody else to agree. When three different copies
of Riplox, from three different places, have reported the same link, it is
hidden for everyone.

WHAT XNIPERBUILDS MAY DO. Hide a link, stop the Board taking new ones, or close
it altogether - at any time and without notice. The Board is a free extra
inside a free program, and nothing here promises that it will exist tomorrow or
that anything posted to it will stay reachable. Riplox does not host, store or
supply any video posted there. A link is an address, and everything in section
2 about what you download applies just as much to something you found on the
Board.
